About Us

Built on Trust, Backed by Standards

Get to know the team and principles behind Grand Shine Cleaning's approach to home and business care.

Our Story

A cleaning company built on trust

Grand Shine Cleaning was founded to bring a more dependable, detail-focused standard to home and office cleaning. What started as a small local team has grown into a trusted service across the Millbrook area, built almost entirely on repeat clients and referrals.

Every cleaning professional on our team is trained to the same standard, so whether it's your first booking or your fiftieth, you know exactly what to expect.

Our Mission

Giving you back your time

Our mission is to give homeowners and businesses their time back by delivering cleaning services that are consistent, careful and genuinely dependable — without cutting corners.

We measure success not by how fast a job is finished, but by how our clients feel walking back into their space.
